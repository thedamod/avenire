6.3 · The MOS capacitor

Inversion and the threshold voltage

With zero gate bias the surface holds majority holes and no channel exists between the n+ source and drain regions.

Only when VGS exceeds Vth does the electron concentration at the surface exceed the hole concentration in the bulk: the surface is inverted and a conducting channel appears.

Thinner oxides give a lower Vth for the same doping.
