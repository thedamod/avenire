# Avenire

Avenire is a study companion that brings your material together, keeps it organized, and helps you make sense of it. Save what you are studying and let Ava, your AI assistant, turn it into useful work that is ready when you need it.

## Your study material, in one place

Capture open tabs, papers, PDFs and videos in one go, or save material through the browser extension. Avenire also supports sources such as web pages, documents, audio and Markdown. Related material is organized into notebooks, so you can find it again without sorting everything by hand.

Search across your sources, write notes alongside them, and pick up where you left off. Your library is available across the web, desktop and mobile.

## Meet Ava

Ask Ava questions about your material and get answers grounded in your sources, with citations to help you follow them back. Ava can also help you explore ideas through charts, interactive figures and short explainer videos.

Ava's `explainer` skill combines a restrained visual language with numerical modelling. Simple physical systems can use simulations; machines can use mechanisms and cutaways; abstract concepts can use analogies to make causal relationships clearer. Widgets stay concise, with explanations and derivations in chat.

With Ava Cloud, you can ask Ava to work in the background: bring together papers into a report, prepare summaries and notes, or make a quiz from what you are studying. Schedule work for later and return to the results when they are ready.

### Explain what's on your screen

On desktop, Ava can request the visible screen when needed to answer your question. It can explain what you are looking at with annotations placed directly over the material and interactive figures alongside it. Screen context is requested on demand; capture and presentation support depend on the desktop environment.

## You stay in control

Ava proposes changes to your files for you to accept or skip. Capturing pages behind a sign-in is optional, and deleted notes and notebooks stay in Trash for 30 days.
